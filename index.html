Steps
    How to load it on the Edge 530
    1. Download the .fit file.
    2. Plug the Edge into a computer with USB. Copy the file into Garmin/NewFiles.
    3. Unplug. The Edge moves it into Garmin/Workouts on boot.
    4. On the Edge: Training → Workouts, pick it, and ride. With your trainer paired as a Smart Trainer (ANT+ FE-C), the Edge controls resistance in ERG mode.

    No computer handy? Upload the .fit to a calendar service that syncs to Garmin Connect (e.g. intervals.icu) and it will reach the Edge wirelessly.
